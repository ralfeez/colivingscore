How to use this dashboard

Screening a market

The three headline numbers — 65+ population, how many live alone, and 10-year growth — tell you whether a county has enough seniors, whether those seniors are socially isolated (the core Silver Living guest profile), and whether that pool is expanding or contracting. Run the same lookup across several counties to compare markets side by side before committing to a location.

Reading the age chart

All demographic metrics on this dashboard measure the 65+ population — that is your current addressable guest. The gray 55–64 bars are shown for context only: seniors in that bracket will cross into your target demographic over the next decade. Counties with large 55–64 cohorts today represent stronger long-term bets even if their current 65+ numbers look modest.

The IL cost benchmark

Independent living communities are the most direct alternative a Silver Living guest would consider — same age group, similar social motivation, but a very different price point and level of service. The state median shown here tells you what that alternative costs. A Silver Living home priced meaningfully below this range has a clear value proposition to the market; one priced above it does not.
